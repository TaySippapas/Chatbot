# Quillmark Bank Customer Policy Handbook

_Fictional company. All policies, names and numbers are invented for a portfolio project._

## Accounts

### [POL-ACC-01] Everyday Checking monthly fee and waivers

Everyday Checking carries a monthly maintenance fee of $7. The fee is waived for any statement cycle in which the account receives at least $500 in total direct deposits, or keeps a minimum daily balance of $1,500 or more. Customers aged 24 and under, and customers aged 65 and over, never pay the monthly fee. If the fee was charged in a cycle where a waiver condition was met, the customer can request a refund; Quillmark refunds the fee within 3 business days once the qualifying deposit or balance is confirmed. If the fee was charged only because a direct deposit arrived one day after the cycle closed, Quillmark refunds it as a courtesy once per 12 months.

### [POL-ACC-02] Overdraft fees, the Overdraft Cushion and the Fix-It Window

Quillmark charges an overdraft fee of $29 per item that overdraws an Everyday Checking account by more than $50. Overdrafts of $50 or less (the Overdraft Cushion) carry no fee. No more than 3 overdraft fees can be charged per business day. Under the Fix-It Window, if the customer brings the account back to a zero or positive balance by 11:59 p.m. local time on the next business day, all overdraft fees from that event are refunded automatically. Customers can opt out of overdraft coverage in the app under Settings > Overdraft; transactions that would overdraw the account are then declined at no cost.

### [POL-ACC-03] Quillmark Saver interest and withdrawals

Quillmark Saver pays a base rate of 2.40% APY. In any month with no withdrawals, a bonus of 0.60% APY is added, for a total of 3.00% APY that month. Interest is calculated daily and paid on the last business day of each month. Up to 6 withdrawals or outgoing transfers per month are free; each additional withdrawal costs $5. There is no minimum balance, but the account must be funded with at least $25 within 30 days of opening or it is closed automatically.

### [POL-ACC-04] Mobile check deposits and funds availability

Mobile check deposits are limited to $5,000 per business day and $15,000 per rolling 30 days. For deposits made before 8:00 p.m. on a business day, the first $225 is available the next business day and the rest within 2 business days. For accounts open fewer than 30 days, Quillmark may hold mobile deposits for up to 7 business days. Customers are told about any extended hold by app notification and email, including the date funds will be available. Customers must keep the paper check for 14 days after deposit, then destroy it.

### [POL-ACC-05] Closing an account

Customers can close an account in a branch, by phone, or in the app once the balance is $0 and no transactions are pending. Accounts closed within 90 days of opening are charged a $25 early closure fee, except when the closure is caused by a Quillmark error or a documented fraud event. Customers are responsible for redirecting recurring payments and direct deposits; Quillmark returns any deposit received after closure to the sender within 5 business days. Any remaining positive balance is mailed by check to the address on file within 10 business days.

### [POL-ACC-06] Dormant accounts

An account with no customer-initiated activity for 18 months is classified as dormant. Dormant accounts cannot send outgoing transfers until reactivated. From 24 months of inactivity, a dormancy fee of $3 per month applies to accounts with a balance under $250. Reactivation is free and happens with any customer-initiated transaction or by verifying identity in a branch or by phone. Quillmark mails a written notice 30 days before an account becomes dormant.

### [POL-FEE-01] Standard fee schedule

Standard service fees: domestic outgoing wire $25; international outgoing wire $45; incoming wires free; cashier's check $10; stop payment order $30 (free when placed online for a single item); paper statements $2 per month (free for customers aged 65 and over); replacement debit card free with standard delivery or $15 for expedited 2-day delivery; returned deposited item $12.

## Cards

### [POL-CRD-01] Spark Card terms

The Spark Card has no annual fee and a variable purchase APR of 21.49%. Cash advances carry a 27.49% APR and a fee of 5% of the advance (minimum $10). The foreign transaction fee is 3%. The late payment fee is $32, or $41 if another late payment occurred in the previous 6 billing cycles. Spark cardholders earn 1% cash back on all purchases, credited automatically each statement.

### [POL-CRD-02] Horizon Rewards Card terms

The Horizon Rewards Card has a $95 annual fee, charged on the account anniversary, and a variable purchase APR of 23.24%. It earns 2 points per dollar on dining and travel and 1 point per dollar on everything else. There is no foreign transaction fee. Points are worth 1 cent each as a statement credit, or 1.25 cents each when redeemed for travel through the Quillmark Travel portal. Points expire only if the account has no purchases for 36 consecutive months, or immediately if the account is closed with unredeemed points. The annual fee is refunded in full if the card is closed within 30 days of the fee posting. Late fees follow the same schedule as the Spark Card ($32, or $41 for a repeat late payment within 6 billing cycles).

### [POL-CRD-03] Payment due dates, grace period and interest

Card statements close on the same day each month, and payment is due 23 days after the statement closing date. If the full statement balance is paid by the due date, no interest is charged on new purchases. Cash advances accrue interest from the transaction date with no grace period. Payments received by 5:00 p.m. local time on the due date are credited the same day; payments made in the app are credited instantly.

### [POL-CRD-04] Lost, stolen and replacement cards

Lost or stolen cards should be reported immediately in the app or through the 24/7 card line. Locking a card in the app (Card > Lock card) is temporary and can be reversed; reporting a card lost cancels it permanently. Replacement cards ship within 1 business day and arrive in 5-7 business days by standard mail. Expedited 2-day delivery costs $15, except for Horizon Rewards cardholders, for whom it is free. A digital card number is available in the app immediately after reporting, so customers can keep using mobile wallets.

### [POL-CRD-05] Credit limit increases

Cardholders become eligible to request a credit limit increase after 6 consecutive on-time payments. Requests can be made once every 4 months and use only a soft inquiry, which does not affect the customer's credit score. Quillmark also reviews accounts automatically every 6 months. Requests are usually decided within 2 business days. Accounts with a payment more than 30 days late in the past 12 months are not eligible.

### [POL-CRD-06] Late fee courtesy waiver

Quillmark waives one card late fee per rolling 12 months at the customer's request, if the account had no other late payments in the prior 12 months and the past-due amount has been paid. The waiver does not change how the payment was reported: a payment that was 30 or more days late is still reported to Meridian Credit Registry. Fees caused by a Quillmark error are reversed under the Service Disruption Remediation policy and do not use up the courtesy waiver.

## Disputes and fraud

### [POL-DSP-01] Disputing a card transaction

Customers can dispute a credit or debit card transaction within 60 days of the statement date on which it first appeared. Disputes filed in the app or by phone receive a provisional credit within 10 business days while Quillmark investigates. Investigations are completed within 45 days, or within 90 days for foreign transactions and for accounts open fewer than 30 days. If the dispute is resolved in the merchant's favor, the provisional credit is reversed with 5 business days' notice. Disputes filed after the 60-day window are reviewed only if the customer provides evidence of fraud or a Quillmark error.

### [POL-DSP-02] Unauthorized transactions and customer liability

Credit card customers have $0 liability for unauthorized transactions. For debit cards and Everyday Checking, liability is $0 if the customer reports the unauthorized transaction within 2 business days of learning of it, and up to $50 if reported later but within 60 days of the statement on which it first appeared. After 60 days, the customer may be responsible for unauthorized transfers that timely reporting would have prevented. Quillmark issues provisional credit for unauthorized debit transactions within 5 business days of the report.

### [POL-DSP-03] QuickSend person-to-person transfers

QuickSend is Quillmark's instant person-to-person transfer service, limited to $2,500 per day. Completed QuickSend payments are generally not reversible. If a customer sends money to the wrong recipient by mistake, Quillmark asks the recipient's bank to return it but cannot guarantee recovery. If a customer was tricked into sending money by someone impersonating Quillmark, the Fraud Review Team reviews the case within 30 days and decides on reimbursement case by case. Quillmark employees will never ask a customer to send a QuickSend payment to 'protect' their account.

### [POL-DSP-04] Service Disruption Remediation

When a Quillmark system outage, processing error, or mailing error harms customers, Quillmark: (1) reverses duplicate or erroneous transactions; (2) automatically reverses any overdraft, late, or returned-item fees that resulted, within 10 business days of the incident being resolved; and (3) corrects any resulting negative credit reporting within 5 business days. Customers who incurred documented third-party costs (for example, a landlord's or merchant's late fee) can claim reimbursement of up to $100 with receipts. Customers who contact Quillmark about the disruption may also receive a $25 goodwill credit at the agent's discretion. Fee reversals under this policy never count toward courtesy waiver limits.

## Loans

### [POL-LON-01] Personal loans

Quillmark personal loans range from $2,000 to $40,000 with terms of 24 to 72 months. APRs range from 8.99% to 24.99% based on credit profile, including a 0.25% discount for enrolling in autopay. Origination fees range from 0% to 4% of the loan amount and are deducted from the disbursed funds. There is no prepayment penalty. Funds are disbursed within 2 business days of approval to a Quillmark account, or within 4 business days to an external account.

### [POL-LON-02] Late loan payments

Personal loan and home loan payments have a 15-day grace period. Payments received after the grace period incur a late fee of $35 or 5% of the scheduled payment, whichever is less. A loan payment is reported as late to Meridian Credit Registry only once it is 30 or more days past due. If autopay fails because of insufficient funds, Quillmark retries once after 3 business days.

### [POL-LON-03] Steady Path hardship program

Customers with a documented loss of income, medical emergency, or natural disaster can apply for Steady Path for personal loans, home loans, and credit cards. Loan options are up to 3 months of reduced (interest-only) payments, or skipping up to 2 payments in a 12-month period, with skipped payments added to the end of the loan. For credit cards, the APR is reduced to 9.99% for 6 months and the card is frozen for new purchases. No late fees are charged while enrolled, and accounts that meet Steady Path terms are reported to Meridian Credit Registry as current. Decisions are made within 5 business days of receiving documentation.

### [POL-LON-04] Home loan escrow analysis

Quillmark performs an escrow analysis for every home loan each March. A shortage under $100 is added to the next monthly payment. Larger shortages are spread over the next 12 payments, or the customer may pay the shortage in full to keep the monthly payment unchanged. Surpluses of $50 or more are refunded by check within 30 days; smaller surpluses stay in the escrow account. Customers can request a re-analysis if their property tax or insurance bill changes, and Quillmark completes it within 15 business days.

## Collections

### [POL-COL-01] Delinquency and charge-off timeline

At 30 days past due, the account is reported as delinquent to Meridian Credit Registry. At 60 days past due, card accounts are blocked from new purchases. At 120 days past due, card balances and personal loans are charged off and transferred to Quillmark Recovery Services (QRS), the bank's in-house collections team. Quillmark may place or sell charged-off debt with a third-party agency only after at least 180 days past due and after sending the customer 30 days' written notice. Customers can bring an account current at any point before charge-off to restore normal status.

### [POL-COL-02] Collections contact standards

Quillmark Recovery Services and any agency collecting for Quillmark may contact customers only between 8:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m. in the customer's local time, and may make no more than 7 call attempts in any 7-day period. Collectors may not contact a customer's employer except once to verify employment, and must stop calling a workplace number if asked. Customers can request written-only communication at any time, effective within 2 business days. Quillmark can recall accounts from agencies that violate these standards.

### [POL-COL-03] Debt validation

Within 30 days of the first collection notice, a customer may request validation of the debt in writing, by phone, or in the app. Collection activity is paused until Quillmark sends a validation letter, which it must send within 15 business days. The letter shows the original creditor, the last 4 digits of the account number, the charge-off date and balance, and an itemization of interest and fees since charge-off. If Quillmark cannot validate the debt, collection stops and any negative reporting by Quillmark Recovery Services is deleted.

### [POL-COL-04] Settlements and payment plans

For charged-off accounts, Quillmark Recovery Services offers lump-sum settlements, typically between 40% and 70% of the balance, and payment plans of up to 36 months with no additional interest. Every settlement is confirmed in writing before the customer pays. Once a settlement is paid, the account is reported to Meridian Credit Registry as 'settled for less than the full balance' within 30 days; accounts paid in full are reported as 'paid in full'. Forgiven amounts over $600 may be reported to tax authorities.

## Credit reporting

### [POL-CRR-01] Credit reporting and corrections

Quillmark reports account status to Meridian Credit Registry monthly, on each account's statement closing date. Customers who believe something was reported inaccurately can request a correction in the app or by phone. Quillmark investigates within 30 days. If the information was reported in error, Quillmark sends a correction to Meridian Credit Registry within 5 business days of confirming the error and mails the customer a confirmation letter. Meridian Credit Registry typically updates the customer's file within 10 days of receiving the correction.

### [POL-CRR-02] Goodwill removal of late payments

Quillmark does not remove accurately reported late payments, with one exception: a customer may request a one-time goodwill removal of a single 30-day late payment if the account has at least 24 consecutive months of on-time payments since that late payment. Only one goodwill removal is granted per customer for the life of the relationship. Late payments of 60 days or more are never removed through goodwill.

## Digital banking

### [POL-DIG-01] Online and app sign-in

After 5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts, online and app access is locked for 30 minutes. Customers can unlock immediately by verifying their identity with a one-time code sent to the phone on file. If the phone number on file is out of date, identity must be verified by calling customer service or visiting a branch with a government ID. Quillmark supports the two most recent major versions of its app; older versions may be blocked from signing in.

### [POL-DIG-02] Alerts and statements

E-statements are the default for all accounts and stay available in the app for 7 years. Paper statements cost $2 per month, waived for customers aged 65 and over. Customers can set balance, transaction, and payment-due alerts by push notification, text, or email. Payment-due reminders are sent 5 days before the due date by default.

## Customer service

### [POL-SVC-01] Complaint handling and escalation

Quillmark acknowledges every complaint within 2 business days and aims to resolve it within 15 business days. If a complaint is not resolved within 15 business days, or the customer is unhappy with the outcome, it can be escalated to the Quillmark Customer Advocacy Office, which responds within 10 business days. If the customer is still dissatisfied 30 days after the original complaint, they may refer it to the National Consumer Banking Ombudsman (NCBO), an independent body. Every final response letter includes the NCBO's contact details.

### [POL-SVC-02] Identity verification and impersonation scams

Quillmark will never ask a customer for their full password, a one-time passcode, or a card PIN by phone, text, or email. Quillmark may call customers about suspected fraud, but customers can always hang up and call back on the number printed on the back of their card. A caller who asks for a one-time passcode or asks the customer to move money to a 'safe account' is not Quillmark.

### [POL-SVC-03] Branch and support hours

Branches are open Monday to Friday 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. and Saturday 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. Phone support is available 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. every day, and the card line for lost or stolen cards and fraud is open 24/7. In-app chat is staffed 24/7 for card and fraud issues and 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. for everything else.
